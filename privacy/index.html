Privacy Policy

PicFrame Manager · Effective October 18, 2026

PicFrame Manager is a companion app for PicFrame digital photo frames, made by Matthew Phipps. The short version: the developer does not collect any of your data. The app has no accounts, no analytics, no advertising and no tracking. Everything it handles goes only to your own photo frame and to the cloud storage account that the frame's owner set up.

The app sees only the photos you pick in the iOS photo picker; it does not read the rest of your photo library. The camera is used only to scan a frame's pairing QR code, and nothing from the camera is saved or sent. The app asks for local network access so it can find and talk to a frame on your home WiFi.

Who else is involved

  • Your photo frame. It belongs to you or to the person who invited you. It keeps a list of paired phones and a log of pairing and security events. The developer has no access to it.
  • Koofr (cloud storage). Uploaded photos are stored in the frame owner's own Koofr account, under that account's terms and privacy policy. The developer has no access to it.
  • Tailscale. When you are away from home, the app reaches your frame through an encrypted (HTTPS) address the frame owner set up with Tailscale. Tailscale relays the connection; it is not given your photos in readable form.
  • Apple. If you have chosen to share analytics with app developers in your iPhone's settings, Apple may share anonymous crash reports with the developer. You can turn this off in Settings → Privacy & Security → Analytics & Improvements.

No data is sold, shared for advertising, or used to track you across apps or websites.

Keeping and deleting your information

Information on your iPhone stays until you remove it:

  • Unpair a frame to delete its pairing details and cloud sign-in from your phone and remove your phone from the frame's list.
  • Settings → Clear All Data deletes everything the app has stored on your phone.
  • Deleting the app removes the app's data. Some iOS Keychain items can remain after an app is deleted, so use Clear All Data first if you want everything gone.

Photos you uploaded stay on the frame and in the frame owner's cloud storage until they are deleted. The frame owner can delete any photo from the app or the frame, and can remove any paired phone. If you uploaded photos to someone else's frame and want them removed, ask the frame owner, or email us and we will help you reach them.

Children

PicFrame Manager is not directed to children under 13, and the developer does not knowingly collect information from anyone.

WanderWall

iPhone apps for WanderWall digital photo frames

WanderWall – Photo Frame is for the frame's owner: add photos from your iPhone, organize them into albums, choose what your frame shows, and keep an eye on it from anywhere.

WanderWall Share is for family and friends the owner invites: send photos to their frame from your iPhone.

Both apps require a WanderWall photo frame.
